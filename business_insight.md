Retail Sales Analysis — Business Insights

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, product performance, city-wise sales, and monthly trends.

2. Key Business Insights

Insight 1: Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard reports approximately 5 million in total sales. This KPI helps monitor overall business performance.

Insight 2: Product Performance

Monitor is the highest-performing product by sales, generating approximately 2 million. Office Chair ranks second, with approximately 1.2 million in sales.

Recommendation: Monitor demand and inventory levels for these products to reduce the risk of stock shortages.

Insight 3: City-wise Sales Performance

Patna has the highest sales among the displayed cities, at approximately 0.9 million. Varanasi ranks second, followed by Lucknow.

Recommendation: Analyze customer demand and sales opportunities in the best-performing cities.

Insight 4: Order Volume

The dashboard displays 495 total orders.

Recommendation: Track order volume regularly to understand business activity and support operational planning.

Insight 5: Monthly Sales Analysis

The dashboard includes a monthly sales trend chart. However, the months do not appear in chronological order.

Recommendation: Sort the months correctly from January to December before drawing conclusions about seasonal patterns or monthly sales growth.

3. Business Recommendations

- Monitor the sales performance of top-performing products.
- Improve inventory planning based on product demand.
- Compare sales performance across cities.
- Track total sales and orders regularly.
- Correct the monthly sorting before performing time-based analysis.

4. Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides an overview of retail sales, product performance, order volume, and city-wise performance. These insights can support data-driven business decisions.

Note: Figures are approximate values read from the dashboard. Monthly trends require further validation.
