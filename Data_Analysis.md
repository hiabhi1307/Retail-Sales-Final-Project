Retail Sales Data Analysis Report

1. Introduction

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and understand product performance, sales distribution across cities, and overall order activity.

2. Tools Used

- Power BI for data visualization
- Excel for viewing and preparing data
- GitHub for project documentation

3. Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales: Approximately 5 million

These figures are based on the current Power BI dashboard.

4. Product Analysis

Monitor is the highest-performing product by sales, with approximately 2 million. Office Chair ranks second, with approximately 1.2 million.

Business Recommendation: Monitor sales demand and inventory levels for these products.

5. City-wise Analysis

Patna shows the highest sales among the displayed cities, at approximately 0.9 million. Varanasi and Lucknow are also among the stronger-performing cities.

Business Recommendation: Investigate customer demand in these cities to identify opportunities for growth.
